---
title: "FAQ: Bounties"
description: "Frequently asked questions about bounties on Boundless."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a bounty?">
    A bounty is a task with a reward. The reward is held in escrow until someone completes the work and is selected according to the bounty’s [mode](/contributors/bounties/bounty-modes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the bounty modes?">
    A mode combines an **entry type** (Open, Application light, or Application full) with a **selection** (single claim, where one contributor is chosen, or competition, where several compete and the best wins). See [Bounty modes](/contributors/bounties/bounty-modes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get paid for a bounty?">
    When you’re selected as the winner (or one of the winners), the reward is released from escrow to your Boundless wallet in USDC. You can hold it, use it on the platform, or withdraw it and [convert it to local currency](/reference/converting-usdc) on an exchange.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I disagree with a bounty outcome?">
    You can open a dispute and provide evidence. See [Handle disputes](/help/disputes-and-moderation).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
