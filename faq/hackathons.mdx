---
title: "FAQ: Hackathons"
description: "Frequently asked questions about hackathons on Boundless."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a Boundless hackathon?">
    A time-boxed event where participants build projects and compete for prizes. Cash prizes are held in escrow and released to winners. Some events have [post-event milestones](/contributors/hackathons/post-event-milestones) for extra payouts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is a non-cash prize?">
    A prize that isn't paid in USDC, such as hardware, API credits, an event ticket, or travel. Some hackathons offer these instead of a prize pool, and the hackathon page lists them in words. Non-cash prizes aren't held in escrow. See [Hackathons overview](/contributors/hackathons/overview#non-cash-prizes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who delivers a non-cash prize?">
    The organizer. Once the winners are confirmed, the organizer contacts each winner and delivers the prize directly. Boundless doesn't deliver or track non-cash prizes, so the prize won't appear in your wallet. If you have a question about yours, ask the organizer. The win still counts on your profile and toward your reputation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I participate as a team?">
    Yes, when the event allows it. See [Form a hackathon team](/contributors/hackathons/form-a-team) and [Participating](/contributors/hackathons/join-and-submit).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are winners chosen?">
    Organizers set up a [judging system](/contributors/hackathons/tracks-prizes-judging) with criteria and judges. Submissions are scored and ranked; top projects win according to the prize structure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are post-event milestones?">
    Optional follow-up deliverables (e.g., mainnet deployment) that winners can complete after the hackathon to unlock additional payouts. See [Post-event milestones](/contributors/hackathons/post-event-milestones).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I organize a hackathon?">
    See [Organizing](/organizers/host-a-hackathon). You’ll create the event, choose cash prizes (held in escrow) or [non-cash prizes](/organizers/non-cash-prizes), configure judging, and optionally add post-event milestones.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
