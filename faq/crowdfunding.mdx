---
title: "FAQ: Crowdfunding"
description: "Frequently asked questions about crowdfunding on Boundless."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is crowdfunding on Boundless?">
    Creators run [campaigns](/backers/overview) with a funding goal. Backers contribute USDC; funds are held in escrow and released according to the campaign’s [milestone structure](/backers/milestones-and-releases) when the creator delivers and (if applicable) [community validation](/backers/vote-on-campaigns) passes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I back a campaign?">
    Choose a campaign, enter the amount you want to contribute, and confirm. See [Backing campaigns](/backers/back-a-project).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does the creator get the funds?">
    According to the campaign’s [milestone structure](/backers/milestones-and-releases). The creator submits evidence for each milestone; when it’s approved (and any community validation is met), that portion is released from escrow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is community validation?">
    A process where the community (e.g., backers) can vote or signal on milestone releases or other campaign decisions. See [Community validation](/backers/vote-on-campaigns).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    Refund rules depend on the campaign (e.g., goal not met, cancellation). Check the campaign details and platform policy. Disputes can be raised via [Handle disputes](/help/disputes-and-moderation).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
