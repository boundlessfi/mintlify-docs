---
title: "FAQ: Grants"
description: "Frequently asked questions about grants on Boundless."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a grant on Boundless?">
    A grant is application-based funding. You apply to a [grant program](/organizers/run-a-grant-program); if accepted, you deliver [milestones](/contributors/grants/deliver-milestones) and receive payouts from escrow as they’re approved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What grant types exist?">
    Common types are milestone-based (payouts per milestone), one-time (single payout after delivery), and program-specific structures. See [Grant types](/contributors/grants/grant-types).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get paid for a grant?">
    Complete each milestone and submit for review. When the milestone is approved, that portion of the grant is released from escrow to your wallet. See [Milestone delivery](/contributors/grants/deliver-milestones).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My milestone was rejected. What can I do?">
    Check the feedback from the reviewer. You may be able to resubmit if the program allows. If you believe it was unfair, see [Handle disputes](/help/disputes-and-moderation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I structure milestones?">
    See [Deliver milestones](/contributors/grants/deliver-milestones) for tips on defining clear, verifiable milestones.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
