---

Organizing a hackathon on Boundless involves creating the event, setting rules and prizes, configuring [judging](/contributors/hackathons/tracks-prizes-judging), and optionally [post-event milestones](/contributors/hackathons/post-event-milestones). Prize funds are locked in [on-chain escrow](/concepts/escrow-and-human-review).

## Before you start

- [Set up your wallet](/concepts/wallet-and-balance) and ensure you have USDC for prizes.
- Define your event: theme, duration, tracks, and prize structure.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Set the event name, dates, description, and rules. Define tracks or categories if applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set prizes">
    Define prize amounts and lock them in escrow. Specify how many winners and how prizes are split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure judging">
    Set up the [judging system](/contributors/hackathons/tracks-prizes-judging): criteria, judges, and scoring method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: post-event milestones">
    If you want follow-up work (e.g., mainnet deployment, docs), configure [post-event milestones](/contributors/hackathons/post-event-milestones).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Open registration and announce the hackathon. Manage submissions and judging until the event ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>
