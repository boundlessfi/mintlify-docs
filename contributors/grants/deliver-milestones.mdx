---
title: "Deliver milestones"
description: "How to deliver grant milestones on Boundless, who approves them, and how each payout is released."
---

For a grant, you deliver the work one milestone at a time. You submit evidence for each milestone, and once it's approved the matching payout is released from escrow.

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the milestone">
    Do the work described in the milestone (for example, a deliverable, report, or demo).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your evidence">
    Submit your deliverable (a link, file, or the form the program asks for).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    The program manager or a designated reviewer checks your submission against the milestone criteria. This review happens off-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payout">
    Once your milestone is approved, the organization signs the on-chain claim and the milestone's share is released from escrow to your wallet, usually within about a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each milestone's amount is set when the grant is published (the position's share of the budget, divided across its milestones). See [Grant types](/contributors/grants/grant-types).
</Note>

## If a milestone is rejected

If your submission doesn't meet the criteria, the milestone can be rejected. Rejections are final for that milestone, so review the milestone description carefully before you submit, and talk to your program manager about how to proceed. If you believe a rejection was unfair, see [Disputes and moderation](/help/disputes-and-moderation).

## Tips

- Match your submission to the milestone description to avoid delays or rejections.
- Keep your evidence clear and self-contained so a reviewer can approve it without follow-up.
