Bounties on Boundless are tasks with a reward. Funders lock payment in [on-chain escrow](/concepts/escrow-and-human-review); claimers complete the work and receive the reward when conditions are met.

## Key concepts

- **Bounty:** A task plus a reward (in USDC or other assets), held in escrow until completion.
- **Mode:** How a bounty is set up, its entry type and how the winner is selected. See [Bounty modes](/contributors/bounties/bounty-modes).

## For claimers

- Browse open bounties and filter by skill, reward, or mode.
- [Apply for bounties](/contributors/bounties/apply) according to the rules of each bounty.
- Build [reputation](/concepts/reputation) by delivering quality work.

## For creators

- [Create bounties](/organizers/post-a-bounty) by defining the task, reward, and mode.
- Fund escrow; once the task is completed and verified, funds release to the winner(s).
