title: "Applying for bounties"
description: "How to find and apply for bounties on Boundless."
---

To claim a bounty, you submit work (or a claim) according to the bounty’s rules and mode. This guide covers how to find bounties and apply.

## Before you apply

- [Set up your wallet](/concepts/wallet-and-balance).
- Optional: [Build your reputation](/contributors/get-paid/build-your-reputation).
- Read the bounty description, deadline, and [mode](/contributors/bounties/bounty-modes).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a bounty">
    Browse the Bounties section. Filter by skill, reward size, or mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rules">
    Check requirements, deliverables, and how the winner is chosen, whether one contributor is picked or several compete and the best wins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your claim">
    Submit your work or application as specified (link, file, form) before the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for resolution">
    The creator or judge reviews submissions and selects the winner(s). Funds release from escrow to the winner(s).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Deliver exactly what the bounty asks for to avoid disputes.
- For best-submission bounties, present your work clearly so judges can evaluate it fairly.
