> **TL;DR:** Boundless is a Web3 collaboration platform where every payment is protected by on-chain escrow and every contribution builds your on-chain reputation.

## Welcome

Whether you're a designer hunting for bounties, a researcher applying for grants, a developer joining a hackathon, or a creator launching a crowdfunding campaign, this is your guide to [Boundless](https://www.boundlessfi.xyz).

**No blockchain experience? No problem.** [Boundless](https://www.boundlessfi.xyz) is designed to feel like a normal web app: sign up with email, the platform manages your balance (no wallet or seed phrase), and payments use escrow powered by Boundless's on-chain escrow.

<Note>
  You can browse **bounties**, **hackathons**, **grants**, and **crowdfunding** (Projects) today, and take part in bounties end to end. Some flows for the other pillars are still rolling out. Payments are in **USDC**; to convert to local currency you use an [exchange](/reference/converting-usdc). Withdrawing funds requires [KYC](/start-here/verify-your-identity) (powered by [Didit](https://didit.me/)).
</Note>

## Core concepts

Before diving into a specific module, understand the foundation:

| Concept                                                  | Why it matters                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [On-chain escrow](/concepts/escrow-and-human-review) | Every payment is locked until work is verified. No trust required.                         |
| [Reputation system](/concepts/reputation)         | Your on-chain track record unlocks bigger opportunities across all modules.                |
| [Wallets and accounts](/concepts/wallet-and-balance)        | Boundless manages your balance: no seed phrase or wallet app. Passkey sign-in coming soon. |
| [Credits](/concepts/credits)                           | Bounty-only currency: spend to take part in bounties; refilled monthly by tier.            |

## Quick start paths

**I want to earn money:**

1. [Set up your account](/start-here/create-your-account) (about 5 minutes)
2. [Find an opportunity](/contributors/find-opportunities), a bounty, hackathon, or grant
3. [Complete KYC](/start-here/verify-your-identity) when you're ready to withdraw

**I want to fund a project:**

1. [Create an account](/start-here/create-your-account)
2. [Launch a campaign](/backers/create-a-campaign) and define your milestones
3. Escrow is powered by Boundless's on-chain escrow contracts

**I want to hire or run events:**

1. [Set up your account](/start-here/create-your-account)
2. [Post a bounty](/organizers/post-a-bounty), [host a hackathon](/organizers/host-a-hackathon), or [run a grant program](/organizers/run-a-grant-program)
