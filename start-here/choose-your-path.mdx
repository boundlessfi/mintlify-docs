## Not sure yet?

Most people start by earning. Create your account, then browse what's live:

1. [Create your account](/start-here/create-your-account) (about 5 minutes)
2. [Find opportunities](/contributors/find-opportunities) across every pillar
3. [Verify your identity](/start-here/verify-your-identity) when you're ready to withdraw

You can switch between roles at any time, the same account and reputation follow you everywhere.
